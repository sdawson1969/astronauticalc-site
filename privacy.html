Privacy Policy

AstronautiCalc — last updated 9 September 2026

AstronautiCalc does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data.

The app performs all calculations entirely on your device. It contains no networking code and makes no connections to any server, including ours. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs.

Values you enter, and results the app produces, stay on your device. If you copy a value to the clipboard, it is handled by iOS in the ordinary way and is never sent anywhere by this app.

Because no data is collected, there is nothing to request access to, correct, or delete.
